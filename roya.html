Brand Strategy

Brand Identity

2024

Infrastructure,
made visible.

Roya.ai gives developers instant, on-demand GPU access — no queues, no overhead, at lower cost than major cloud providers. The brand needed to feel as direct and capable as the product itself.

The halftone sphere logomark encodes the core idea: raw compute made visible — each dot a unit of power, organized into something precise and trustworthy. Source Code Pro as the primary typeface grounds the identity in the terminal world where Roya.ai actually lives.

Built for the
terminal.

The visual system extended across print and digital — vertical posters, business cards, brand decks. Each touchpoint carrying the halftone sphere and the spare, monospaced voice of Source Code Pro.

The palette — terracotta, teal, cream, and black — drew from a world older than cloud computing, suggesting the permanence of infrastructure alongside the immediacy of a command line.
