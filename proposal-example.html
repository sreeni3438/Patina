Proposal for Marlo

End-to-end design for Marlo's
budgeting platform.

A dedicated full-time senior product designer, backed by a design lead, to redesign Marlo's core experience and design the new budgeting feature end to end — on a flat monthly retainer.

Background

Marlo is a spend management platform for fast-growing companies. Built feature by feature over four years, the interface works but no longer reflects the quality of the company behind it.

You're now introducing budgeting — letting finance teams set, track, and enforce budgets across the organisation — and it's the moment to address the wider experience alongside it. The challenge is onboarding finance leads into the model, giving teams clear visibility into spend against budget, and designing controls they trust rather than work around.

Scope of work

Four areas of work, covering the budgeting feature end to end and the surrounding experience it depends on.

Budgeting onboarding

The setup flow where finance leads define budgets, allocate them across teams, and configure rules. The first impression of budgeting, and where adoption is won or lost.

Spend visibility & reporting

Dashboards and reports that show spend against budget in real time — by team, category, and period — so finance and team leads always know where they stand.

Budget controls & approvals

The rules, limits, alerts, and approval flows that enforce budgets in practice — designed to guide behaviour without creating friction that teams route around.

UX audit of existing flows

A review of the current product beyond budgeting — surfacing friction, inconsistencies, and quick wins, prioritised and addressed within the same engagement.

Deliverables

Approximately 40 high-fidelity screens across the engagement, plus prototypes, a design system, and engineering-ready handoff.

~40 high-fidelity screens

Production-ready designs covering every meaningful state — default, empty, loading, error, and edge cases — across the budgeting feature and redesigned flows.

Clean, annotated Figma files — specs, states, and behaviour documented — set up to work with Figma MCP so engineers can pull components directly into their workflow.

Design system & component library

A documented set of components, patterns, and tokens that the team can extend and reuse beyond this engagement.

UX audit & recommendations

A prioritised list of issues and opportunities across the existing product, with recommendations and designs for the items addressed during the engagement.

Two design reviews per week

Standing sessions to review work in progress and align on direction, so feedback is continuous and there are no surprises.
