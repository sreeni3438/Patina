Give it back.

Freedom is built on a simple idea: managing your team should never feel like a second job. Our brand work positioned Freedom as the tool that removes friction from running a team — starting with a name that says everything.

The visual identity reflects that clarity. A single-line bird that speaks to freedom, flight, and effortlessness. The acid green cuts through a sea of corporate blue, signaling that Freedom is a different kind of HR company — one that actually wants to give you yours back.

Weightless,
everywhere.

The campaign extended the brand into outdoor advertising — bold, direct, and unmistakable. "HR that finally feels weightless" worked at every scale, from a shelter panel to a city billboard.

The lime green ensured Freedom stood out in any environment. No clutter, no jargon — just the promise of a tool that gets out of your way.
