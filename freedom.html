Freedom

Serro is an AI workstream reporting tool built for engineering and product teams. Status updates are time-consuming, scattered across emails, Slack threads, and meeting notes — Serro aggregates it all and drafts your reports automatically.

We designed Serro to feel effortless. The interface needed to be minimal enough to disappear, letting the AI do the heavy lifting while keeping humans firmly in control of the final output.

Structured Reporting

Every workstream update follows a consistent structure — workstream name, type, target date, engineers, designers, PRD links, risks, path to green, confidence, and milestones. Serro pre-fills what it knows and flags what it doesn't, so your reports are always complete and audit-ready.

The report editor surfaces exactly what stakeholders need to see. Rich-text formatting, structured fields, and inline team tags combine into a single coherent document — ready to confirm and send in one click.

Every AI-generated field cites its source. Hover any populated value to see exactly which document, email, or transcript it came from — building trust without slowing anyone down.

The AI panel sits alongside your report — always accessible, never intrusive. Ask Serro to fill in a missing field and it searches your connected sources, surfaces the answer, and populates the form with a single click.

The Outcome

Once a report is confirmed, Serro can schedule it to send automatically on any cadence — every Monday, twice a week until launch, or on a custom schedule you define. Status reporting becomes a system, not a task.

Teams using Serro reduced time spent on status reporting by over 60%. Reports that used to take 45 minutes are generated in under 5 — with full source attribution so every stakeholder stays aligned.
