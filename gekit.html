Infrastructure
you don't see.

Gekit sits beneath every AI agent, quietly making them faster. When an AI agent receives a prompt, it typically queries a vector database, a SQL database, and a third-party API just to build its context window. This runtime processing adds seconds of latency before the model even begins to generate a response.

Our strategy work positions Gekit as foundational infrastructure — not a feature, but the layer everything else depends on. The identity draws from archival, print-era aesthetics: blues and reds that echo technical diagrams and annotated drawings, a visual language that feels rigorous, deliberate, and built to last.

Layered Planes

The logomark encodes Gekit's core behavior: moving from noise to precision. Layered planes shift upward and rightward — each surface more resolved than the last — signaling a system that is continuously improving. What begins as fragmented data evolves into clear, actionable memory.

The upward, rightward trajectory encodes momentum. The mark reads as something studied and constructed, not captured — reinforcing Gekit's role as infrastructure that exposes how things work, not just what they produce.
