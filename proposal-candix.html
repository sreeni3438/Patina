Proposal for Candix

End-to-end design for Candix's
dynamic pricing layer.

One design lead and one dedicated full-time senior designer. Two engagement options below — same scope, different rhythm.

The brief

Candix connects engineers with companies through curated, mutual intros — no recruiters, no search, no social layer. With 40+ placements and Mistral as a flagship client, the platform has proven its model. Now you're mid-rebuild, adding a dynamic pricing system that uses market competition to keep match quality high as you scale.

The design challenge is the company side: onboarding companies into a budget model they don't yet understand, building visibility into how their spend translates to matches, and doing all of it in a way that earns trust before companies feel control.

What we're designing

The pricing feature end to end — from the moment a company sets a budget to the moment they see results and understand why.

Budget onboarding

The flow where companies set their brief, configure spend, and calibrate match criteria — including uploading example profiles. The critical moment where trust is either built or lost.

Pricing transparency

How the dynamic model is explained at the right moment. Companies need to understand that competitive demand drives price — without feeling like they're bidding blindly or ceding full control.

Budget tracking dashboard

Where companies see spend over time, match activity, and ROI. The interface that turns a hiring manager into a repeat customer — because the value is visible, not just assumed.

Trust architecture

Patterns across the product that reduce friction when companies hand over budget control — copy, progressive disclosure, sensible defaults, and signals that Candix is working for them.

Overall UX audit

A review of the end-to-end company experience — not just the pricing layer. Surfaces friction, inconsistencies, and quick wins across the product that can be addressed as part of the same engagement.

Engagement options

Two ways to structure the 30 days. Same designer, same scope — different rhythm and level of flexibility.

Day 11 onwards

Design — iterative

Designs are built, reviewed, and revised in short cycles rather than saved for a big reveal. We'll drive the pricing feature through to high-fidelity and use the bandwidth to audit other flows, surface friction across the platform, and pick up broader UX work as it comes up.
