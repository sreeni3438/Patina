Proposal for Alleviate

Rebrand and Website for Alleviate

What we're designing

Brand identity

Logo and wordmark, colour system, typography, iconography, imagery direction, and the visual language that ties them together.

Website design

A full redesign of the marketing site — home, product, pricing, about, and the supporting pages. Information architecture, wireframes, then high-fidelity design across desktop, tablet, and mobile, including empty, hover, and loading states.

Design system for the site

A reusable component library behind the pages — buttons, cards, navigation, forms, type and spacing scales.

Brand guidelines and asset kit

A practical guidelines doc — usage rules, do's and don'ts, tone — plus organised source files, exported assets, and annotated design handoff, so the team can keep the brand consistent after the engagement ends.

How we'll run it

Step 1 — Week 1

Discovery

A kickoff covering positioning, audience, and goals. We audit the current brand and site.

Step 2 — Week 1

Identity direction

Logo, colour, type, and visual language, developed as routes. Sitemap and page structure for the new site.

Feedback session 1 of 3 — Week 1

Direction review

We walk through the identity routes and site structure together, talk through your notes on the website design, and work out the copy and direction with you before we build on it.

Step 3 — Weeks 2–3

Brand implementation and website

The identity applied across the site. All five pages designed in high fidelity for desktop and mobile, plus the component library behind them and the brand strategy doc. This closes out week 3.

Feedback session 2 of 3 — Week 3

Design review

We go through the full site with you page by page, work through your notes together, and refine the copy and layouts until it feels right.

Step 4 — Week 4

Supporting assets and guidelines

The final week: social assets and Google templates, plus the brand guidelines and the documented component library.

Feedback session 3 of 3 — Week 4

Final review

We go over the supporting assets and guidelines with you and make any last adjustments before the files are handed over.

Step 5 — Week 4

Handoff

Organised source files and exported assets, with annotated files handed to your developers.
