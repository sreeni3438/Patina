Proposal for Backops

A complete rebrand for Backops —
website, sales content, marketing.

One design lead and one dedicated senior designer. Two engagement options below — a fixed package or an ongoing retainer.

What we're designing

The rebrand end to end — from the identity system itself to every surface a customer or prospect actually touches.

Brand identity

Logo and wordmark, colour system, typography, iconography, imagery direction, and the visual language that ties them together. The foundation everything else is built on.

Website

A full redesign of the marketing site — home, product, pricing, about, and the supporting pages. Structure, messaging hierarchy, and high-fidelity design across desktop and mobile.

Sales content

The pitch deck, one-pagers, case studies, and proposal template your team sends every week — rebuilt on the new identity and set up as editable templates, not one-off files.

Marketing materials

Social and ad templates, email layouts, event and print collateral, and the reusable content formats marketing needs to publish consistently without redesigning from scratch each time.

Brand guidelines and asset kit

A practical guidelines doc — usage rules, do's and don'ts, tone — plus organised source files and exported assets, so the team can keep the brand consistent after the engagement ends.

The process includes two feedback sessions in total.

Step 1 — Weeks 1–2

Discovery and direction

A kickoff to understand positioning, audience, and how sales and marketing actually work today. We audit every existing asset, then develop the identity direction — logo, colour, type, and visual language, shown in context rather than in isolation.

Feedback session 1 of 2

Direction review

One working session to review the direction and choose a route. Consolidated notes from your side, one revision pass from ours — then the direction is locked and everything downstream is built on it.

Step 2 — Weeks 3–6

Application

The locked identity applied across every surface — the full website in high fidelity, the sales deck and one-pagers, the marketing templates. Designed as one pass, without review calls in between.

Feedback session 2 of 2

Application review

The second and final session. We walk through everything together, collect one consolidated round of notes across all surfaces, and apply them in a single revision pass.

Step 3 — Weeks 7–8

Guidelines and handoff

Final polish, brand guidelines, editable templates for sales and marketing, organised source files, and exported assets. Annotated files handed to your developers, ready to build.
