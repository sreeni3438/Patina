Quill

Quill is an AI meeting assistant that joins your calls, listens, and turns every conversation into structured notes — decisions, action items, and sourced claims — without anyone taking a single manual note.

We designed Quill to disappear into the workflow. The challenge was building something powerful enough to be trusted, yet minimal enough to never get in the way.

Every Meeting, Captured

Connect your calendar and Quill quietly joins every eligible meeting. It listens, structures what was said, and has clean notes ready before the call even ends — decisions surfaced, action items assigned, nothing lost.

Setup takes under a minute. Connect Google Calendar, Outlook, or Zoom and Quill starts joining automatically — no extensions, no manual invites, no friction.

Before a call begins, Quill builds a briefing — pulling context from Salesforce, prior emails, and past meetings so you walk in informed. Nothing to prepare. Everything already there.

Every meeting becomes a structured document — a summary, key decisions, and action items with owners and due dates. Quill infers the structure from the conversation, not a template.

Every decision and action item links back to the exact moment it was said. Hover any item to see the quote, who said it, and when — so nothing gets disputed after the fact.

The Outcome

Action items don't live in notes — they go directly into Jira, Notion, or Linear with a single click, pre-filled with the context Quill already captured. The meeting ends and the work is already in the right place.

Teams using Quill reclaimed hours of post-meeting admin every week. Notes that used to take 30 minutes to write are generated instantly, with full attribution so every stakeholder stays aligned.
