Freedom

Freedom is an HR platform built to make compliance and people management feel effortless. The brand needed to counteract the heaviness of the category — clean whites, generous space, and a typographic system that makes even complex workflows feel approachable.

Gekit

The Gekit mark represents layers. Stacked planes signal how data exists across multiple levels — fragmented, distributed, and structured over time. The form is minimal and precise, surfacing only what's necessary while hinting at the complexity beneath. Built for engineers, but legible to anyone who's ever had to make sense of a system.

Roya

Roya is a GPU cloud platform built for teams pushing the limits of compute. The brand needed to feel as serious as the silicon it runs on — precise, dense, and technical without being cold. Every detail references the physical reality of chips: heat, structure, and the quiet power of optimised silicon.

Aris

Aris is a GPU infrastructure platform for AI workloads. The brand speaks to engineers who think in silicon — direct, architectural, and stripped of anything unnecessary. Form follows function, just like the hardware it abstracts.
